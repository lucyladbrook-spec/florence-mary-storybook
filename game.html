        Answering Our Big Question

        "How did a lamp and herbs change hospitals?"

        🕯️ Florence's Lamp

        Florence checked on soldiers at night and made hospitals clean, fresh, and hygienic so germs could not spread.

        🌿 Mary's Herbs

        Mary used traditional Jamaican herbs, warm food, and kind care right on the battlefield to help hurt soldiers heal.

        Together, Florence Nightingale and Mary Seacole showed the world that hospitals need both clean, safe wards and kind, loving care!
